# Post Type: UGC Comment-Reply Testimonial

**Format:** Selfie-style talking head, framed as a reply to a real (or staged-native) comment.
**Length:** 60–90s organic master → 45–60s paid cutdown.
**Funnel stage:** MOFU/BOFU conversion.
**Best for:** Products whose core objection is *"I already know how to do this / I don't need an app for this."* The speaker is a credible expert who repositions the product from teacher to **cognitive offloader** — it removes decisions, not ignorance.

**Reference example:** [Ladder fitness testimonial breakdown](../examples/ladder-fitness-ugc-testimonial.md)

---

## The 6-beat structure

| # | Beat | Timing | Psychological job |
|---|------|--------|-------------------|
| 1 | Social Proof Hook | 0:00–0:06 | Comment-reply sticker bypasses the "ad filter"; opens as conversation, not pitch |
| 2 | Authority + Core Problem | 0:06–0:26 | Speaker qualifies as an expert, then names the *emotional* problem (burnout, decision fatigue) — not a skill gap |
| 3 | Situational Pain Point | 0:26–0:46 | A concrete, relatable scenario where the product became necessary (travel, bad equipment, chaos) |
| 4 | Hard Result | 0:46–0:54 | One quantitative, believable metric that validates the emotional claims |
| 5 | Everyman Reframe + Feature Walkthrough | 0:54–1:12 | Lower the ego ("I'm not special"), then translate features into friction removed |
| 6 | Emotional Outro | 1:12–1:26 | Identity/age anchor locks the demographic; close on *anticipation*, not discipline |

## Script template

Fill the brackets; keep the sentence rhythms conversational — write it like it'll be said, not read.

> **[Beat 1 — Hook]** "I get comments just like this every single day about [PRODUCT], and honestly, it changed my life too."
> *(Overlay: native comment-reply sticker with a real customer quote.)*
>
> **[Beat 2 — Authority + Problem]** "Now, I've been [DOING THE THING] since [AGE/YEAR] — I'm fairly familiar with [DOMAIN]. But how it changed my life is [EMOTIONAL SHIFT: fell back in love with X / took my mind off everything]. I'm a busy [IDENTITY], and [THE THING] was becoming [distracting / a chore / another decision]."
>
> **[Beat 3 — Situational pain]** "When I found [PRODUCT], it was honestly because [SPECIFIC SCENARIO: traveling for work / newborn at home / bad equipment], and I needed [THE JOB: a trainer in my ear / a plan handed to me] to keep me on track."
>
> **[Beat 4 — Hard result]** "And it did exactly that. I [METRIC: dropped 20-something pounds] in [TIMEFRAME: like three months]."
>
> **[Beat 5 — Everyman + features]** "I'm a busy person, and I know everybody on this app is just as busy as me — I'm not a special snowflake. But I can [OPEN CONTEXT: get to a gym], open my phone, and [ZERO-FRICTION MOMENT: the workout's ready for me]. It [FEATURE→BENEFIT ×3: tracks my weights, counts my reps, times my rest] — everything in one app."
>
> **[Beat 6 — Outro]** "So it's so much more than a [CATEGORY] app. It changed my life. It [EMOTIONAL PAYOFF] and got me [PEAK STATE] at [AGE]. And honestly, every single day I can't wait to [ANTICIPATION HOOK: see what my coach has in store for me tomorrow]."

## Casting & voice rules

- Speaker must plausibly be the **target avatar** (busy professional, 28–45), and must state an age or tenure on camera — it's the demographic lock.
- Expertise claim comes *first* ("since I was 14") so the endorsement can't be dismissed as beginner enthusiasm.
- One ego-lowering line is mandatory ("I'm not a special snowflake") — it converts authority into relatability instead of distance.

## Shooting specs

- Selfie-angle mid-shot, handheld or loosely propped — **keep the micro-shake**; polished static framing reads as an ad.
- Soft natural light, aspirational-but-real setting (tidy apartment > studio > gym).
- Single continuous take is fine; hand gestures carry the visual rhythm.

## Edit & optimization checklist

- [ ] Comment-reply sticker overlaid natively for the full hook (beat 1).
- [ ] Audio QC end-to-end — stitching/looping glitches kill retention; mask any splice with a jump cut or 10–15% punch-in.
- [ ] B-roll overlays at proof moments: app UI screen-record on the feature walkthrough, transformation photo on the hard result (2–3s each).
- [ ] Cut ums/ahs/dead air aggressively for the paid cutdown (target 45–60s cold-traffic version; keep the 60–90s master for organic/retargeting).
- [ ] Captions on by default; keyword the metric and the age.

## Variant briefs (same skeleton, new inputs)

Rotate these to keep the format fresh without changing the structure:

1. Swap the **situational pain** (travel → new parent → shift worker → injury comeback).
2. Swap the **hard result** (weight → strength PR → consistency streak → time saved).
3. Swap the **hook comment** — pull a different real customer quote each time.
