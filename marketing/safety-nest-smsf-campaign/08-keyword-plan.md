## Trigger Moment 1 — pre-setup researcher (30–50 men deciding)

- **Group A should-I (high vol / med-high comp):** smsf vs industry fund; is an smsf
  worth it; smsf pros and cons; disadvantages of smsf; how much super do you need to
  start an smsf; smsf minimum balance. *Industry funds bid defensively here.*
- **Group B how-to (high/high):** how to set up an smsf; smsf setup checklist; smsf
  trust deed; corporate trustee vs individual; smsf bank account; how long does it
  take to set up an smsf.
- **Group C cost (high/high):** smsf setup cost; smsf annual fees; how much does it
  cost to run an smsf; smsf accountant fees; smsf cost vs industry fund.
- **Group D provider (avoid paid):** best smsf provider; esuperfund review; stake
  super review — provider brand wars, very high CPC, zero insurance intent.
- **Group E rollover 🥇:** rollover super to smsf; transfer super to smsf; how long
  does it take to transfer super to smsf; partial rollover to smsf; close old super
  fund after rollover; smsf rollover form; smsf esa.
- **Group F insurance-adjacent 🥇 (the open lane):** what happens to my insurance if
  i move to an smsf; what happens to insurance when you rollover super; lose
  insurance rolling over to smsf; keep insurance in old super fund; leave money in
  old super fund for insurance; partial rollover keep insurance; minimum balance to
  keep insurance in super; does an smsf need insurance; arrange insurance before
  closing super fund; smsf insurance comparison.
- **Group G motivation feeders (audience-building for YT/Display):** buy property
  with smsf; use super to buy investment property; smsf loan; buy bitcoin with
  super; smsf etf.
- **PAA verbatim:** "What happens to my insurance when I roll over to an SMSF?";
  "Is an SMSF worth it?"; "What is the 5 rule for SMSF?"; "Can I set up an SMSF
  myself?"
- **Forum language:** "SMSF? Worth it or not?"; "a minimum of $200k is often
  suggested"; "looking for forums where people discuss SMSF without the selling
  aspect" *(distrust of providers — plain-spoken insurer wins)*.
