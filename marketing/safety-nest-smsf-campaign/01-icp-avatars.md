# Safety Nest — SMSF Campaign: Kennedy-Style ICP & Customer Avatars

> Methodology: Dan Kennedy's Magnetic Marketing approach — **Message → Market → Media**,
> in that order. We define the *market* (avatar) first, craft a *message* that enters the
> conversation already happening in their head, and only then pick *media*.
> Facts marked **[RESEARCH]** are filled from the verified research fact base
> (`04-research-fact-base.md`).

## The core insight (the "conversation in their head")

A person rolling their super out of an APRA-regulated fund into an SMSF typically
**forfeits the group life / TPD / income-protection cover attached to the old fund**.
Almost nobody warns them at the moment it happens; the accountant who sets up the SMSF
is usually **not licensed to advise on insurance** and so says nothing specific.
Result: a newly-minted SMSF trustee — often the family's primary breadwinner, often
with debt and dependants — is walking around **uninsured without knowing it**, while
also being **legally required** (SIS Reg 4.09) to at least *consider* insurance in the
fund's investment strategy. **[RESEARCH: confirm mechanics, timing, exceptions]**

That gap — *"you just lost your safety net and no one told you"* — is the campaign.
Hence the brand fit: **Safety Nest**.

## Avatar 1 — "The Control-Taker" (primary: SMSF establisher / new trustee)

### Demographics [RESEARCH: verify against ATO trustee statistics]
- Age: TBC (hypothesis: 35–55, with new-establishment median trending younger)
- Household: couple, often with dependent children and a mortgage
- Balance: TBC median at establishment
- Occupation: business owners, professionals, senior managers, property investors
- Location: Australia-wide, skew metro

### Psychographics
- **Drives:** control, distrust of big institutions/industry funds, belief they can do
  better than default super, property/direct-shares ambition, tax optimisation
- **Identity:** "I'm the kind of person who takes charge of my own money."
- **3am fears:** getting the compliance wrong; ATO penalties; being the family's single
  point of failure; "what happens to my family if I'm not here"
- **Frustrations:** jargon, being sold to by institutions they just escaped, forms
- **Watering holes:** [RESEARCH: forums, publications, podcasts, Facebook groups]

### Kennedy avatar questions
| Question | Answer |
|---|---|
| What keeps them up at night? | Compliance risk; being uninsured is *latent* — they don't know it yet (problem-unaware for our product) |
| What are they afraid of? | ATO trouble; family left exposed; making a rookie mistake |
| What are they angry about, and at whom? | Fees and opacity of big super funds |
| Top daily frustrations | Admin burden of the new SMSF |
| Trends in their business/life | New SMSF establishment, rollover in progress or just completed |
| What do they secretly desire most? | To be *right* about going self-managed; to look competent |
| Built-in bias in decision-making | DIY-minded, research-heavy, comparison shoppers, allergic to salesmen |
| Who else sells to them, and how? | SMSF admin platforms, accountants, property spruikers **[RESEARCH]** |
| Who else has tried and failed? | Generic life insurers with generic messages |

### Awareness state (Schwartz overlay Kennedy uses)
- **Problem-unaware → problem-aware** is the money move. They know they left their old
  fund; they do NOT know the insurance died with it. The ad's first job is the reveal.

### The message that matches this market
- Lead with the reveal, not the product: *"Rolled your super into an SMSF? Your life
  insurance probably didn't come with you."*
- Then compliance leverage: *"Your investment strategy is legally required to consider
  insurance (SIS Reg 4.09). Here's the 10-minute fix."*

### Trigger moments (when to reach them)
1. Researching SMSF setup (pre-establishment) — intercept before rollover
2. Just established / just registered ABN & rolled over (0–90 days) — the hot window
3. Annual audit/investment-strategy review — auditor flags insurance consideration

## Avatar 2 — "The Trusted Technician" (channel: accountant / SMSF administrator / adviser)

### Profile [RESEARCH: verify licensing limits + numbers]
- Suburban accounting practice partner or SMSF-specialist administrator
- Sets up N SMSFs per year; trusted-adviser status with clients
- **Licensing constraint:** unlicensed accountants generally *cannot* recommend
  insurance products — but CAN identify the gap and refer. That constraint is our
  wedge: we make referral compliant, easy, and valuable.

### Their 3am
- Fear: a client dies uninsured after an SMSF *they* set up → blame, reputation, even
  liability. Fear of stepping over the AFSL line.
- Desire: look thorough; add value beyond compliance; new revenue or reciprocal referrals.

### Message
- *"You set up the SMSF. Who's making sure your client didn't just cancel their life
  insurance? A compliant referral path that protects your client — and you."*

### Offer shape (B2B)
- Co-branded "SMSF Insurance Gap Checklist" for their client-onboarding pack
- Compliant referral workflow (no advice required from them)
- CPD-eligible webinar: "Insurance and the SMSF investment strategy: what unlicensed
  accountants can and can't say" **[RESEARCH: CPD rules]**
