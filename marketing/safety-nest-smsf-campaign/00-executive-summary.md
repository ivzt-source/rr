# Safety Nest — SMSF Paid Digital Campaign: Executive Summary

*Research and strategy package, July 2026. Built with Dan Kennedy's Magnetic Marketing
method (Market → Message → Media) on an adversarially-verified research fact base.*

## Your assumptions, tested

| Assumption (as briefed) | Verdict |
|---|---|
| SMSF establishers are an ideal Safety Nest avatar | ✅ **Confirmed and strengthened** — record 48,464 new funds in 2025, median new-trustee age 46, largest cohort 35–44 on $100k–$150k, 68% two-member (couples) funds, avg establishment balance only ~$363k, and just ~13–15% of SMSFs hold member insurance. 26% of new SMSFs report an unmet advice need on insurance. |
| Leaving a super fund loses your life insurance | ✅ **Confirmed — and sharper than briefed.** Cover doesn't transfer, AND the standard workaround (leave a small balance in the old fund) fails by statute: SIS Act s68AAA auto-cancels cover after 16 months without contributions unless the member contributes or lodges a written opt-in. ASIC's REP 824 (Nov 2025) names unreplaced insurance a key SMSF-establishment risk. |
| Accountants typically help set up SMSFs | ✅ Confirmed — and since the accountants' exemption repeal (1 July 2016) most are legally UNABLE to recommend replacement insurance (the limited-licence regime collapsed ~88%). They can make disclosed "mere referrals" — which is exactly the B2B product. |
| Addressable market = owner + the team who set it up | ✅ Confirmed as a two-avatar campaign: ~85,000+ new trustees/yr (plus 670k+ existing funds), and the accountant/administrator channel — for whom a compliant referral path solves a real liability/service gap. |

## The third avatar — established trustees (quickest to cash)

Added July 2026 (fact base: `04c`). The stock dwarfs the flow: **~536k funds are
5+ years old** (average fund age 13.5 years), **~79% have no adviser**, and the
honest core is the **~370k wholly-accumulation funds (average member age 52)**.
ASIC surveys show 19% never considered insurance at setup and 30% have no
arrangements if something happens. Every one of these funds must answer the
insurance question annually (Reg 4.09, auditor-checked) — and nobody markets to
that moment. Offer discipline: the offer is always the **insurance piece** (gap
check / cover transfer / old-fund check) — never a commoditised "strategy review."
The anti-churn "don't cancel — transfer" mechanic (takeover terms preserve original
underwriting) converts qualified prospects without new medicals. Targeting cut-off:
accumulation phase, under ~58 — premiums and product entry-age caps make 60+ a
dishonest sell. Verdict on "quickest to cash": **supported, with that targeting
condition** — no setup lag, an annual forcing event, and no gatekeeper adviser.

## The one-sentence strategy

Own the message nobody in the market currently runs as paid advertising — **"your SMSF
rollover quietly cancelled your life insurance, even if you thought you'd kept it"** —
delivered to new trustees inside their 16-month s68AAA window via verified Google/Meta
funnels, and to their accountants as a compliant referral kit that protects both them
and their client.

Competitive check confirmed the position is open: NobleOak and Real Insurance sell
D2C SMSF cover generically, Insurance Watch owns the comparison SERP, the advised
insurers hide behind PDFs — nobody leads with the gap.

## Before spending a dollar (launch gates)

1. **Google Financial Services Verification** (G2 Risk Solutions AFSL check, ~5 days)
   and **Meta AU financial-products verification** — both mandatory for AU insurance
   ads; start immediately.
2. Pull real AU keyword volumes (Keyword Planner/Ahrefs, AU geo) — no public data
   exists; expect A$20–50 CPCs on commercial terms.
3. Legal sign-off on the copy bank against RG 234 (9 June 2026) and the general-advice
   warning; confirm anti-hawking consent flows in the lead forms and referral kit.
4. Confirm product economics (premium, retention, margin) to set CPL/CAC targets.
5. Re-verify the statutory quotes (s68AAA, Reg 4.09(2)(e)) against primary sources —
   research pass was proxy-blocked from ato.gov.au/asic.gov.au and relied on
   search-index text plus secondary corroboration.
