# Safety Nest — SMSF Campaign Plan v2 (Simplified Master)

*July 2026. This is the executable version — one document a media buyer and creative
team can run from. Detail lives in docs 01–06 and 08; verified facts in 04/04b/04c.
Accountant B2B channel: PAUSED (direct-to-consumer only).*

---

## 1. Who we want

**Men 30–50, Australia, anywhere in the SMSF journey.** They underwrite well, stay on
the book for decades, and their premiums grow with age. Their wives/partners are the
emotional co-decision-makers (68% of SMSFs are couples' funds) — creative speaks to
him, protects them.

Two entry moments, same person:
- **Moment A — setting up / just set up** (0–16 months): median age 46 and falling;
  largest cohort 35–44 on $100–150k. A statutory clock is running on the insurance
  he left behind.
- **Moment B — set up years ago, on autopilot** (5+ years, ~536k funds, ~79%
  unadvised): accumulation phase only, under ~58. Nobody has ever answered the
  insurance question for his fund.

Excluded: 60+, pension phase, "retiring soon" signals.

## 2. What we say (message architecture)

**Core message:** *When you took control of your super, your life insurance didn't
come with you — and even if you left some behind, a 16-month clock is running on it.
Check your gap in minutes.*

**USP:** SMSF insurance is ALL we do. Your accountant legally can't recommend cover;
generalist insurers don't understand your structure; we're the specialist for the
672,000 funds everyone else forgot.

**Emotional drivers (rotate — fear is ONE lane, not the whole campaign):**
1. **Fear — ATO/registration:** $6,600-per-trustee penalties, 45% non-complying tax,
   fund delisted from Super Fund Lookup. Used as proof/urgency, never as the offer.
2. **Fear — family exposure:** the nest egg isn't a safety net — shares and property
   don't pay out on a Tuesday; policies do. Illustrative story (A5, labelled) allowed.
3. **Pride/control:** validate the fund, isolate the gap — "The fund's fine. One
   clause is missing." Never imply negligence.
4. **Nostalgia (video):** "Saturday Morning" 80s-cartoon concept — the hero who never
   lost his shield.

**Urgency (all true, RG 234-safe):** the 16-month s68AAA clock on recent rollovers;
age-banded premiums (~$39–50/mo per $500k at 50 → ~4× at 60); health-dependent
insurability ("cover is bought with health, paid with money").

**The statistics fear ad (defensible build):** "11,687 SMSFs established in the March
2026 quarter (ATO). Industry estimates suggest fewer than 1 in 5 ever hold life cover
for their members (Cooper 2010: <13%; 2024 est: ~15%). That's roughly 9,000+ new funds
— and the families behind them — likely without the safety net their old fund
provided." *(Compliance-rate claims are NOT supportable — this is the claimable
version. Dollar figures only as labelled illustrative arithmetic.)*

## 3. The offers (insurance-only — no audits, no reviews, ever)

| Offer | What it is | Best for |
|---|---|---|
| **Gap Calculator** ⭐ | Already built. Free, fast, no-jargon; output = the gap in dollars AND a record the trustee can file | Everyone; every CTA lands here |
| **"Email your accountant" template** | Pre-written email: "Was member insurance ever set up or reviewed for our fund?" Accountant legally can't recommend → answer boomerangs to the specialist | Moment B; unsure-what-they-have |
| **Cover Transfer Assessment** | "Don't cancel — transfer." Takeover terms carry original underwriting, served waiting periods, expired exclusions | Anyone with existing/stale cover |

## 4. Where and how (channels)

**Google Search — harvest intent (40%).**
Own the open lane first: insurance-adjacent and keep-old-cover clusters ("what
happens to my insurance if I move to an smsf", "keep insurance in old super fund",
"inactive super insurance cancelled") — only one insurer competes there. Then
rollover-mechanics terms (the exact moment cover lapses), then selective head terms.
Avoid provider brand wars entirely. Full lists + negatives: `08-keyword-plan.md`.
**"smsf insurance calculator" has zero incumbents — own it.**

**YouTube + Display — find the audience by behaviour (30%).**
Custom segments from what they SEARCH (setup, rollover, strategy-template, property/
crypto-in-super feeders) and SITES they visit (SuperGuide, PropertyChat, admin
platforms, ATO SMSF pages). Demo layer 30–50 skew male. "Saturday Morning" 60s
pre-roll + 6s bumpers; gap-explainer 60–90s. All CTAs → calculator.

**Meta — broad, let the algorithm work (20%).**
Advantage+/broad with the offer doing the targeting (no SMSF interest exists).
Optimize to calculator completions, not raw leads. Creative is the targeting:
gap-reveal hooks, the accountant-email angle ("the email your accountant is waiting
for"), Saturday-Morning cutdowns, statistics ad.

**Retargeting (10%).**
Calculator abandoners → finish-the-check; completers → quote/transfer CTA; content
readers → calculator. 15s glitch cut + urgency bank.

**Compliance gates before any spend:** Google Financial Services Verification (G2RS)
+ Meta AU FPS verification (AFSL) + RG 234 (June 2026 edition) pre-flight per asset
(`05-compliance-guardrails.md`).

  thumb-stop test before full animation).
